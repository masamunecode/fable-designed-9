Four out of five miles should feel easy.

We made a shoe for those. Soft underfoot, steady at the heel, and in no hurry at all.

Shop Lope, $150 → What’s in it

Most running shoes are sold on how fast they’ll make you. This one won’t make you faster. Not directly.

Lope is for the runs in between: the Tuesday five, the Sunday long one, the slow jog that shakes out yesterday’s workout. It’s soft where the road is hard and steady where tired legs get sloppy, and by the second mile you’ll have stopped thinking about your feet.

Go easy on the easy days. The fast ones tend to look after themselves.

Built to take it easy

A lot of foam, a wide base, and nothing on it that asks you to speed up.

  1. 1

    Good-to-Soft foam

    38 mm of nitrogen-infused foam under the heel, tuned to soak up the road instead of firing you down it.

  2. 2

    A late rocker

    The curve starts under your toes, so the shoe rolls you forward without hurrying you along.

  3. 3

    Room up front

    One-piece mesh and a wide toe box, for feet that swell somewhere around mile nine.

  4. 4

    A padded collar

    Thick at the heel and the tongue, the way shoes used to be. There’s nothing to break in.
