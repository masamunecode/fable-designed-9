How we roast

Eleven minutes, watched the whole way.

A five-kilo drum is small enough to steer by smell and sound, so that’s how we steer it. We roast light enough that you can taste where a coffee grew, and long enough past first crack that it’s still sweet in a moka pot.

5 kg
in the drum at a time. About seventeen bags, plus a cupful we taste before anything ships.
5 days
is the oldest a bag gets before it leaves the shop.
1 lot
per farm, per harvest. When it’s finished, it’s finished until next year.
