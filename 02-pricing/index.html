Questions, answered plainly.

Something we missed? Write to hello@dogear.app and a person will reply.

Is Pocket really free forever?

Yes. It isn’t a trial and there’s no cap on notes. We make money when people outgrow two devices, not by holding their writing hostage.

Can I try Desk before paying?

You get 14 days with no card on file. When the trial ends you choose: pay, or drop back to Pocket with every note intact.

Who counts as a person on Library?

Anyone who can edit. People you invite only to read or comment are guests, and guests are free. Add someone mid-cycle and you’re charged only for the days that are left.

Can you read my notes?

Notes are encrypted in transit and at rest on every plan. On Desk and Library you can turn on end-to-end encryption per notebook. We can’t read those, and we can’t recover them if you lose your key.

Can I bring my notes from another app?

There are importers for Evernote, Notion, Apple Notes, Bear and any folder of Markdown files. Tags, attachments and links between notes come across with them.

How do refunds work?

Monthly plans can be refunded within 14 days of a charge, yearly plans within 30. Email us and it’s done. There’s no form to fill in.
